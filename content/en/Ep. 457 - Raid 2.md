# Raid 2

"Oh! This is quite interesting—"

Just as Ainar reached out to pick up the item, I shouted,

"Stop!!!"

I pulled Ainar away from the item and let out a sigh of relief.

"...W-What?! I didn't do anything wrong!!"

Ainar, confused and flustered, immediately started defending herself.

Of course, I wasn't planning to scold her.

"W-What is this thing?!"

Ignorance is not a crime.

Well... at least not for barbarians.

"It's No. 9999, Beginner's Luck."

When I revealed the item's name, only Amelia reacted.

"...We've obtained a valuable item from the start."

Yes, it's valuable indeed.

Money can't buy this.

"What is it? What's so special about it?"

"Hmm... It's one of the 109 treasures of the labyrinth. Didn't you read any books when you were young?"

"...I didn't ask you."

I didn't know what the 109 treasures were, but I intervened before they could start arguing.

"Beginner's Luck is a charm item."

In the game, it was classified as a 'Charm'.

"A charm...?"

It seemed like an unfamiliar term, so I explained the concept of a charm, and Erwen, with her years of experience as an explorer, quickly understood.

"Ah, so it's an item that activates its effect just by having it in your possession?"

"That's right."

There were twelve Numbered Items classified as charms, and their effects didn't stack, even if you had multiple charms.

Only one charm could be active at a time.

"And Beginner's Luck has a special characteristic. It binds to its first owner."

"...Like the 'Orb of Fire' you mentioned before?"

"Yes."

"...B-But I didn't know!! I swear! Please believe me!!"

Ainar panicked when she heard the word 'bind', but we ignored her and continued our conversation.

"So what's its effect?"

Ah, that.

It's simple.

This Numbered Item isn't particularly helpful in combat. It doesn't increase your item level or give you any stat boosts.

But...

"It increases the probability of an essence dropping from the first monster you slay."

"Ah... I see... It's a fascinating item!"

Erwen seemed to accept it, but Ainar was disappointed.

"What? That's it? It doesn't even guarantee an essence drop! And it only works on the first monster?!"

It was an understandable reaction.

They didn't know the exact probability.

According to my (self-made) statistics, Beginner's Luck increased the essence drop rate by about 5%.

And it wasn't a multiplicative increase.

It was a fixed bonus.

For example, the Guardian's essence had a drop rate of 33%, so with Beginner's Luck, it would become 38%.

The effect seemed insignificant...

"So how much does it actually increase the drop rate?"

...but it depended on the monster.

There was a huge difference between 33% becoming 38%...

And 0.01% becoming 5.01%.

"It's about a 1 in 20 chance."

I gave them a rough estimate, and Ainar, Erwen, and even Amelia were surprised.

"R-Really?! One in twenty?!"

Even if they didn't know the exact drop rate, explorers knew one thing from experience.

Essence drop rates were abysmal.

I added,

"Ah, of course, that's for 9th-grade monsters. The drop rate is slightly higher for higher-ranked monsters."

Higher-ranked monsters had a slightly higher base drop rate.

Some 3rd-grade monsters even had a 5% drop rate.

Well, they were incredibly difficult to find, but...

"It's definitely worthy of being one of the 109 treasures."

"Yes, it's a remarkable item. Even if it only works on the first monster, with this drop rate, you could make a fortune just by hiring a mage and going around slaying monsters."

"Bjorn... I'm sorry. I didn't know it was that valuable..."

Ainar suddenly apologized.

She seemed dejected that she had almost touched the item without knowing its true value. I ignored her and focused on the next issue.

We had to decide who would get the item.

It was a bound item, so we couldn't sell it.

"As long as we're bonded, we don't have to be the one who slays the monster, right...?"

"It would be most efficient if the person with the lowest level used it."

Amelia was right.

The most efficient way to use the item was to give it to the lowest-level member.

Amelia was level 8, and Erwen, Ainar, and I were level 7.

"Auyen, what's your Soul Rank?"

"...Y-Yes? Ah, yes! It's 6...!"

We could give it to Auyen, who was level 6, and use him as a totem...

'But I can't waste such a valuable item on a non-combatant who can't even join us for real raids.'

I made my decision.

"Ainar, you take it."

Erwen had slain a Bellarios before, but Ainar hadn't.

And most importantly...

"...Huh? M-Me? It's a good item, but... wouldn't it be better if Bjorn used it...?"

I told her,

"You can only equip one charm at a time."

We couldn't waste an equipment slot.

Especially on a bound item that we couldn't even unequip.

"It's better if you have it. So take it, Ainar."

"Bjorn!!!"

Ainar seemed happy with my decision.

'The profit margin would be too small, considering the cost of the test tube.'

The only real reward was the Guardian's essence, and I was planning to give it to Erwen if it dropped, so there was no point in delaying it.

「Character has entered the 3rd-floor Pilgrim's Path.」

We arrived at the 3rd floor and headed straight for the Orc Encampment after passing through the Steelrock Hills.

Our target this time was a monster I had a deep connection with.

The same monster that the Djarwi Clan had been raiding when the Chaos Lord was summoned.

The Orc Hero.

"Oooooh! So I can use [Gigantification] now?! I can finally become a giant too!!"

Ainar was ecstatic when she heard about our next target, but none of us were planning to absorb its essence.

[Gigantification] had a huge penalty without the [Unification] effect from the Undead Engraving.

So we were just going to farm experience points and hope for a Numbered Item drop.

But...

'With Beginner's Luck, there's a chance we might get an essence.'

I couldn't just ignore that possibility.

So I approached every mage we encountered while mining magic stones for the Orc Warlord and asked if they had a test tube.

We were lucky enough to find one.

"Test tube...? I have one, but..."

"Great. If we get an essence, put it in this. I'll pay you for the test tube right away."

"!!!"

My fame made things easier.

The 3rd-floor rift hadn't opened yet.

We resumed mining magic stones.

"Just to be safe, don't come near the altar until I get back. I won't be able to protect you if the Orc Lord spawns."

"The Orc Lord...?"

"It's a 2nd-grade monster."

"A... A 2nd-grade monster?!"

"It's safe as long as you stay away, so don't worry."

"I-I'll be careful..."

'This is a good opportunity to practice our teamwork.'
