# Showdown 1



Lee Hansu's room felt unusually strange today.

'It feels... small.'

I felt a strange sense of claustrophobia in this room that had always been my sanctuary.

And I wasn't even in my large barbarian body.

'Whatever, let's turn on the computer first.'

I quickly dismissed the strange feeling and turned on the computer, logging into the community.

[Long Live Korean Independence] - 0 users online.

I checked the Korean chat room first, but it was empty.

'Hyunbyul isn't here yet?'

I decided to check the forums first.

As expected, the forums were already filled with new posts.

Nonsense posts, advice requests...

I clicked on the most eye-catching post.

[Breaking News: Bjorn Yandel, presumed dead, returns alive.]

Content.

The poster's intentions were clear.

He was so desperate to be the first to post about it that he didn't even bother writing anything in the content section.

But it was the first post, so the comments were flooding in.

[Harbinn: Wow! The Marquis must be shocked, and the royal family must be in an uproar!]

Well, some seemed to be hearing it for the first time.
