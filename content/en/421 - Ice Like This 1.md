# Ice Like This 1

Dragon Speech: Soul Silence.

A cheat-like skill that burns away all MP within range, leading to Soul Exhaustion.

However, it has a weakness.

As I heard from the Dragonkin elder, it can only burn away up to 40% of your maximum MP.

'That's probably why he was saving it until now.'

We had received healing from the priests after escaping the Eye of the Glacier, but MP recovery is a different story.

My MP was already over half depleted, and I had used a considerable amount fighting these guys, using [Gigantification] and spamming [Swing].

That's why I provoked him.

[I'm curious, when will that moment come? The moment when death can't save me?]

[...Crack.]

He was running out of MP.

Of course, I had [Soul Dive], but it has a cooldown. Once I used it, there would be no way to counter another Dragon Speech.

That's why I urged him to use it quickly.

And the result...

"Cough! Hack! Cough...!"

The Dragonslayer was incapacitated.

Hmm, he did struggle whenever he used Dragon Speech in Lalkas's Maze...

But I didn't expect him to be this weakened.

An unexpected gain.

「Character has cast [Soul Dive].」

「Soul Power is regenerated proportionally to the amount consumed.」

「Character has cast [Gigantification].」

On the other hand, I had recovered my depleted MP and was ready to fight again.

"H-how...?"

That dumbfounded expression is a bonus.

"Behel—laaaaaaaaaa!!"

I feel the energy of my ancestor god coursing through my veins.

「Manua Repeles has cast [Iron Fist].」

The bald guy's Iron Fist attack followed, but I just smiled and took the hit.

Hmm, they weren't affected by Soul Silence.

'Well, they would have formed a Bond beforehand if they were planning to use Dragon Speech.'

The Noark frontliners, including the bald guy, weren't affected by Soul Silence.

It seems like the backline was outside the range.

'Thankfully, our team was also outside the range.'

That's why I had sent the frontline back.

It was a secondary measure to block the raid party, but the main reason was to prevent them from being hit by Soul Silence.

'He probably thought he could end it all by taking me down. He must have calculated that the others would be in danger if I was incapacitated.'

That thought made me laugh.

It was ridiculous.

[...You always forced me to sacrifice.]

This is what he calls sacrifice?

If he was really willing to sacrifice, he should have gone all out.

But he was still holding back.

'Good thing he did.'

「Character has cast [Eye of the Storm].」

「The inherent ability of the skill is unlocked by the power of the Transcendent.」

I activated my grab skill and pulled the Dragonslayer towards me.

A strong wind swept through the passage, dragging him closer.

Crack!

I swung my hammer, shattering his left shoulder.

"Aaaaargh...!"

'Damn, I aimed for his head. He dodged.'

Well, there's always next time.

I blocked the bald guy, who was rushing to save the Dragonslayer.

No, it wasn't just him.

「Mike Roymers has cast [Ground Breaker].」

「Reduces damage received by allies within range by half, and takes the remaining damage instead.」

A mace warrior, close to a tank.

「Leah Andes has cast [Wave of Fire].」

「Temporarily increases all fire damage dealt, and gains additional effects when closer to the target.」

A rare close-range ability user.

They must have practiced together often.

Now that the Dragonslayer was down, they were even more aggressive.

「Cale Elbad Jeneger has cast [Earth Spike].」

Their attacks pierced my defenses, tearing through my flesh and shattering my bones.

But...

「Royta Mamender has cast [Greater Heal].」

「Benjamin Orman has cast [Greater Heal].」

「Kelver's Iron has cast the 4th-grade black magic [Mark of Evil].」

「Reduces all regeneration effects on the target.」

The black mage persistently cast curse magic, but it was useless.

「Benjamin Orman has cast [Moonlight Purification].」

「Removes all negative effects on the target.」

Cleansing was easier than cursing.

"Focus on defense!"

We maintained our defensive formation.

This wasn't a raid, it was a siege.

'They can't escape anyway.'

They were trapped.

The monsters were swarming behind them, and the boss monster was climbing up from below.

They would be overwhelmed if they retreated to the 7th floor.

They wouldn't even last a day with their limited food supply.

"Damn it, where did this monster come from...!"

The mace warrior, who had challenged me to a strength contest, was dumbfounded.

"He's pushing us back even with three of us attacking him!"

Ah, they were hoping to break through with just three people?

They were overconfident.

"Repeles! We have to retreat!"

The mace warrior pleaded with the bald guy, but he didn't listen.

"Where can we go?!"

"We have to break through! We can't stay here!"

He knew.

If they retreated, it was game over.

"We just have to kill this one guy!"

"The rest are nothing!"

Their only hope was to break through our defenses.

"Behel—laaaaaaaaaa!!"

But I wasn't going to let them.

"What about Cullen?"

"Can't you see? He's dead!"

'Good, that leaves 11...'

The enemy's numbers had dwindled.

On the other hand, we had no casualties.

The priests were focused on healing me, so even the injured members were mostly lightly wounded.

But...

'This is where it gets tricky.'

I doubled down on my vigilance.

They had realized it too.

That if this continued, they might all die here.

"...To think we would have to risk our lives in a place like this."

Humans are calculating creatures.

That's why, when faced with a life-or-death situation, they choose to give up.

Everything except survival.

'That's the most dangerous state to encounter in an enemy.'

It's like a boss monster's 'berserk' pattern.

Fighting with their lives on the line.

"That monster has retreated!"

"Now's our chance!"

The Noark explorers, seeing me retreat, charged forward with renewed vigor.

「Jun Arsene has cast [Divine Release].」

And...

Kaislan, Raviyen, Amelia.

Whooosh!

If you get hit by them, you die.

"Push him back! Throw him off the cliff—"

"Aaaaaargh!"
