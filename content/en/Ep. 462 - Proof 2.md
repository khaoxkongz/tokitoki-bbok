# Proof 2



My skin was still burning from the heat of the battle.

But the air around me was cold, and the gazes of the Sawtooth Clan members were hostile.

'I didn't expect them to be this aggressive.'

The atmosphere was tense, but I tried to remain calm and analyze the situation.

It wasn't the worst-case scenario yet.

Threats meant they wanted something.

'If they were really planning to fight us, they would have ambushed us instead of coming here and talking.'

Then why were they bluffing?

I had to figure that out first.

I had a hunch, but I needed to confirm it.

"Loot..."

I chuckled and asked,

"What makes you think I looted it from you? Riakis was roaming the floor alone when I encountered him. Isn't that right, Erwen?"

"Yes? Ah, yes! That's right. I didn't detect any other explorers within a 1-kilometer radius. And besides, there are many precedents that state that you can't claim ownership of a monster that's roaming freely."

Erwen provided her testimony, but the dagger user wasn't backing down.

"That applies to ordinary monsters, not Floor Lords."

"So what's your point?"

"We were separated from Riakis during the raid, but that doesn't mean we gave up our claim to him."

Separated during the raid?

Their equipment told a different story.

Their equipment was pristine, even though they had supposedly cleared the 3rd phase.

'They must have been trying to buy time to re-equip.'

The decoy group must have bought them time to retreat and re-equip. They were probably planning to regroup and come back for Riakis.

But they had failed.

The decoy group was nowhere to be seen.

'They must have realized that the decoy group was dead and searched the area. And then they found us and rushed over here.'

I could see the whole picture now.

Well, I couldn't be sure...

But it was the most likely scenario.

"Baron Yandel, you stole the Floor Lord we were raiding. We were risking our lives to slay him, and you just swooped in and took the essence."

"We didn't even know you were there."

"But you still took the essence, even after we warned you."

"What's wrong with that? It's my loot. I earned it."

"But you wouldn't have been able to slay him without our efforts."

He was right about that.

But they were the ones who had summoned the Floor Lord without permission.
