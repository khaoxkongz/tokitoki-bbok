# The Lion’s Return 2



The sky was somewhat gloomy.

Even so, sunlight still streamed through.

A warm energy pushed back the chill that had seeped into our very bones.

There was no bone-chilling coldness, nor hunger gnawing at our stomachs.

“Now… it’s finally sinking in. We really… made it back alive…”

As always, returning to the city after a difficult expedition brought strange sensations.

We were happy to have survived, yet our hearts felt heavy.

Because of those who couldn’t come back with us.

“If only they had endured a little longer…”

If only…

Maybe a few more could have joined us.

“Miss Marone.”

Marone stopped muttering to herself as Sven Parab called her name and gritted her teeth.

Returning to the city didn’t mean it was over.

It was too early to relax. Things might be even harder here than in the labyrinth.

But…

Clench.

She swallowed her complaints.

It wasn’t just because we were currently in the empty mansion where we had gathered for a few days before entering the labyrinth and had to be careful of what we said.

We couldn’t afford to complain.

No matter how high and steep the mountains we had to climb were,

We had to leave those who fought to the very end behind.

“Kaislan, are you feeling alright?”

“I couldn’t be better. But it seems like I was the last one to return.”

Kaislan had been summoned by a priest from the Marquis’s family and had just returned to the drawing room, where the other survivors were gathered.

“Why don’t we go for a walk? It’s a nice day today.”

“I wish we could.”

“You wish we could?”

“You saw the knights outside the drawing room, right? They’re stopping us from leaving.”

Marone grumbled at Akuraba’s explanation.

“Hah! It’s like we’re criminals! We risked our lives to complete the mission, and this is how they treat us? Shouldn’t we be treated like heroes?!”

“Um, Miss Marone…”

“What? Am I wrong?!”

Sven Parab looked at me nervously, but it wasn’t something I had to intervene in.

Their complaints were understandable.

And besides, I had told them to do this.

“Well, we are technically part of the military now. The expedition isn’t officially over until we report to our superiors.”

“Even so, this is too much. Even in the military, they wouldn’t treat us like this.”

“Everyone, calm down. We achieved a great feat! We destroyed the Sky Eye! And after the reports are done, we’ll be rewarded handsomely!”

“Finally!”

James Kala whistled, his face beaming with anticipation.

The conversation naturally shifted to rewards.

They talked about what they would ask for, how much they expected to receive…

All for the benefit of those listening outside.

Our conversation was being recorded.
