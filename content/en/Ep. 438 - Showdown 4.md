# Showdown 4



At first, I was just dumbfounded.

But as I stood there, watching Lee Baekho disappear, the reality of the situation slowly sank in.

“Phew…”

He’s really gone.

That bastard.

‘What was with that ‘poof’ sound…?’

At least I feel a bit lighter now.

I hadn’t even noticed how tired I was until he left.

“…….”

I feel completely drained.

My head is pounding, even worse than when I pulled all-nighters in college, fueled by coffee.

‘Wait, is that why he said his head wasn’t working?’

Ah, whatever.

It doesn’t matter.

Thud.

I collapsed onto the sofa.

I needed a mental break.

But…

‘I can rest later.’

I recited my to-do list out loud, my mind still sluggish.

“…First, I have to review the conversation.”

From our first reunion to his sudden departure.

I replayed every detail of our conversation in my mind.

It’s my usual routine after an incident.

But my head felt like it was about to explode.

I couldn’t remember his exact expressions or the nuances in his tone.

“Ugh, my head hurts…”

It was like trying to accelerate a car with an empty gas tank.

I had the will, but my mind wasn’t cooperating.

So I focused on the most important parts.

[Are you really Bjorn Yandel?]

The bait he threw.

[We’re friends, aren’t we? I can do that much for you. Right?]

His willingness to give up Misha after finding out my identity.

[There’s a button. And if you press it, you can return to Earth.]

His expectations.

And…

[You’re lying.]

His disappointment.

‘There was a lot…’

My thoughts became hazy as I reviewed the conversation.

Maybe he was somewhat satisfied.

He hadn’t gained anything, but…

He hadn’t lost anything either.

At least not today.

‘…Revealing my identity as Bjorn Yandel was part of the plan anyway.’

I had planned to subtly probe his intentions, reveal my identity, and then use our connection to demand Misha’s return.

If that didn’t work, I would threaten him.

I could imply that I was the one who had him unbanned, and that I could have him banned again.

Well, I didn’t even have to mention that.

[Unban me quickly. Or you’ll really die here.]

Considering how desperate he was to return to the community, even after causing chaos in the city, it would have been an effective threat.

‘…But why was he so desperate to be unbanned?’

That thought crossed my mind, but I didn’t dwell on it.

How could I possibly know what he was thinking?

He probably just missed talking to other Koreans.

“…….”

I closed my eyes and let my thoughts wander.
