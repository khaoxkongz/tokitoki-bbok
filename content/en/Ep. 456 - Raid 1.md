# Raid 1



[Dungeon and Stone] is a farming game.

The main appeal of the game is strengthening your character by accumulating experience, absorbing essences, and obtaining equipment.

Typically, you level up your character by gathering experience points and absorbing essences.

Because experience points are only awarded for the first kill of a monster.

If you don't strengthen your character with essences, you can't even reach the next level to farm experience.

'But somehow, I haven't encountered any bottlenecks until reaching level 7.'

Of course, this is largely due to the fact that I absorbed the 7th-grade Corpse Golem, 5th-grade Vampire, and Orc Hero essences early on.

Those essences alone were enough to power through the lower floors, even if I just focused on experience points. The 'Tempered Desire' that allowed me to absorb the Ogre essence in the mid-game also played a significant role.

Ah, the ongoing war also helped.

The 'Bion' essence I obtained through war achievements was originally something I would have started farming at this level.

'So this is the first time I've encountered a real bottleneck...'

Of course, this isn't because I'm lacking in skill. It's just that the only way to get stronger is to go above the 7th floor, but I can't go there because it's a war zone.

Realistically, I could start farming the Bellarios essence right now if my clan was stronger.

Therefore...

"Bjorn! How long do we have to wait here? The monsters here are too weak, it's boring!"

I decided to focus on low-level raids for this expedition.

Looking back, it was the best choice.

We could farm experience points from monsters we would have to hunt eventually, practice our teamwork, and even absorb essences if they dropped.

All four of us had one essence slot left.

"Just be patient. As I said, the essence from this monster is useful for you."

The monster we were hunting had an essence that was particularly useful for Ainar. It wasn't a top-tier essence, but it would make her about 30% stronger.

"Well, even if you say that..."

But Ainar didn't seem convinced.

She probably couldn't believe that a 2nd-floor essence could be helpful at this point.

"It's just a 5th-grade essence! Can't we just keep going up? You need stronger enemies to show off your skills, right? I'll show you how much I've improved!"

Ah... so that's what she was really after.

I ignored her and focused on chewing my jerky.

And after a while...

"Yandel."

Amelia, who had been farming with her clone, called out to me.

And at the same time, Erwen spoke.

"There are people approaching."

"Yeah, she's right. Someone's coming this way. How many—"

"Twelve."

"...."

Damn it, why are they competing over this?

"They'll be here in about two minutes."

"Alright."

We waited, and soon a team of twelve explorers appeared from the darkness.
