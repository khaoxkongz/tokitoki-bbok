# Month 13 (9)

After telling Amelia I’d go back to sleep and sending her away, I lay down on the bed, but as expected, sleep wouldn’t come.

It wasn’t that I wasn’t tired. I had already had a deep sleep once. It was just that, lately, I hadn’t been able to fall asleep properly at night.

During the day, I could keep myself busy and somehow let the time fly by, but at night, that wasn’t possible. Whenever night fell, my head would fill with complicated thoughts.

So today, too, I let out a sigh and stepped outside, beginning my usual walk around the sanctuary.

Despite it being the final night of the festival, the sanctuary was still noisy.

“BETHELRAAAAAAA!!”

“Gwahahahaha!”

As I headed in the opposite direction to avoid the barbarians’ roars, my feet naturally led me to the place I always ended up.

Perhaps they were another reason I kept finding myself coming here.

The cold winds of the thirteenth month swept past, brushing against my skin.

I stopped walking and looked up at the sky. A crescent moon hung there.

Whenever I looked at it, I felt like I was on Earth, yet at the same time it reminded me that I wasn’t.

For a very simple reason.

If you look closely, its shape is slightly different...

‘There are no full moons in Raphdonia.’

No matter when you look up at the night sky, you see the same moon, all year round.

It’s not that the full moon never appeared. It was just extremely rare – something like a total solar eclipse on Earth.

I’m not an astronomer, so I had no idea why it happened, and honestly, it wasn’t something I cared to figure out in this magical, mystical world.

Step.

“Who is it?”

My voice came out sharper than intended, and a barbarian warrior stepped out of the forest, scratching the back of his head in embarrassment.

When I saw who it was, I couldn’t hide my surprise.

“You’re... Vecta, the third son of Kiltau?”

At least it wasn’t Baek-ho, which eased my tension somewhat, though I hadn’t expected to run into him like this.

“Oh! I didn’t think you’d remember my name! It’s an honor, Chief!!” he exclaimed, shouting into the quiet night, clearly moved that I had called him by name.

After helping to resolve the concerns of a novice warrior I had encountered by chance, I felt a little lighter as I headed towards my destination.
