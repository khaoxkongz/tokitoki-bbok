# Raid 5



The Floor Lord.

A field boss that appears on each floor of the labyrinth when specific conditions are met.

It's usually considered a high-level raid, something you attempt only after you're strong enough to clear 7th-floor rifts.

At least, that's the case for the first five floors.

From the 6th floor onwards, the Floor Lord becomes a 'top-tier raid', practically end-game content.

'But this is our first real raid, and it's a Floor Lord...'

Considering that our previous highest-level raid was the 4th-floor 'Doppelganger Forest', this was an absurd situation.

And we only had four people who could fight.

We weren't even a full party, let alone a raid team.

But...

'We have several 3rd-grade essences, so we should be able to land the final blow.'

I made my decision as soon as I saw Riakis in the distance.

It would be foolish not to try.

And besides...

'I made a promise.'

It might sound childish, but I had made a promise to myself.

That if I ever saw him again...

[Grooooaaaaaaaaar!]

...things would be different.

"Yandel, what are we going to do?"

"I'll tank the damage, you guys focus on attacking."

"That's... it?"

Amelia looked at me as if I was insane, but that was the only way to fight Riakis in his 4th phase.

There was no special strategy.

You just had to overpower him.

「The Chaos Lord, Riakis, senses the source of life.」

As soon as we entered his range, Riakis stopped running and turned to face us.

And...

[Grooooaaaaaaaaar!]

He charged at us, roaring.

His footsteps were different from his 1st phase.

Thud, thud, thud!

They were still heavy, but much faster and lighter.

His speed was also incomparable.

His 4th phase was all about agility.

"Everyone, move back."

I sent my teammates to the back and activated [Transcendence] and [Gigantification].

「Size increases proportionally to Strength.」

If it had been his 1st phase, when he was bloated and obese, it wouldn't have been enough... but now that he was lean and muscular, our sizes were similar.

That was one of the advantages of [Gigantification].

「The Chaos Lord, Riakis, senses a great threat from the character.」

It significantly increased my 'Threat' stat, making it easier to hold aggro during raids.

He must have been surprised.

He had probably never encountered an opponent his size before.

"Behel—laaaaaaaaaa!"

I activated [Wild Release], further increasing my Threat stat, and charged at him.

The Giant Shield Charge.

Kwaaang!

The giant barbarian and the giant monster collided, creating a deafening roar.

Screech.

I was pushed back.

It wasn't surprising.

The Floor Lords were notorious for their ridiculous stats.

Thud!

I swung my hammer, activating [Swing]... but he didn't even flinch.
