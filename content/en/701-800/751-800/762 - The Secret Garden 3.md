# The Secret Garden (3)

Lee Baek-ho watched the duel with sparkling eyes.

He pressed his lips together and tilted his head forward in concentration. To Rek Aures, this felt oddly unfamiliar.

“Baek-ho, who do you think will win?”

Lost in his intense concentration, Baek-ho did not respond. Noticing this, Aures repeated the question.

Finally, Baek-ho broke his concentration on the fight and lifted his head to look at Aures.

However, his gaze was slightly off. It was the kind of look that said, ‘What’s wrong with this guy?’

“…Why are you looking at me like that?” Aures asked.

“I was wondering if you were asking seriously.”

“No, I mean, I’m not a god, so how would I know what you’re thinking?”

“You don’t need to be a god to know that. We gave everything we had to kill him. How is that guy supposed to kill someone we couldn’t even take out?”

“B-but, he’s demon eye?”

“I’m Lee Baek-ho. And that old man over there is the Scholar of Ruin.”

“Surviving an ambush and winning a fight are two different things. It may not play out the way we expect,” Aures replied sincerely.

“Wow, I am never betting on horses with you.”

“Betting…?”

“Forget it. Don’t talk to me.”

As if annoyed, Lee Baek-ho cut him off and quickly returned his gaze to the duel.

After a while, Rek Aures cautiously spoke to him again.

He didn’t want to interrupt, especially after being told not to, but he couldn’t bear his curiosity any longer.

“Uh… Baek-ho?”

Without even taking his eyes off the fight, Lee Baek-ho replied mechanically.

“What. Say it fast.”

“Why are you watching so intensely? You said you believed Baron Jandel would win anyway.”

Aures thought Baekho might realize he was acting out of character by asking that question and hesitate over his answer.

“Then shouldn’t I watch intensely? What if our Baron has another card I don’t know about?”

“Ah… I-is that so…?”

Aures was left speechless by Lee Baek-ho’s “isn’t it obvious” tone. In fact, just as Lee Baek-ho said, a ‘hidden card’ did appear.

“A flower crown…? He had an item like that? Judging by how he escaped immediately after using it, it’s probably a mental defence type…”

Baek-ho’s eyes lit up as he hurriedly pulled out a notebook and began scribbling furiously. His pen raced across the page at lightning speed, sharp, excited breaths flaring through his nose. As he wrote, he even licked his lips, like someone savoring a tempting sight.

Aures watched from the side and swallowed down the emotion rising in his throat.

‘No, it’s not right to insult your allies!’

Still, for some reason, he felt a little sorry for Baron Jandel.

Whoosh.

Then—

Everyone stood in silence, staring at what remained of the vice captain, his body smeared across the ground.

“Bjorn Jandel has killed Demon Eye! Bjorn Jandel is now the strongest warrior in the world!”

That was the signal.

“A-Attack!”

The highest-ranking person after the vice-captain shouted the order, and the Noarkans who had been standing by quickly regrouped and charged.

Honestly, it was expected.

The purpose of a ‘duel’ in war is to take out one of the enemy’s capable commanders and boost the morale of your own troops.

That eye-freak probably had the same plan.

To defeat me one-on-one on the stage Lee Baek-ho set up and then gain the upper hand in the following full-scale battle. That must have been Demon Eye’s original plan.

But the moment he lost the duel, the plan became meaningless.

“Alright—let’s go!!”

As the elites of Orcules and the Noark forces clashed from all directions, Lee Baek-ho, who had been observing from the roof, jumped down.

Then—

“Long time no see?”

Hah, tell me something believable.

“By the way, he’s probably not dead yet.”

I just assumed it was a survival-related essence and, since those types of essences have very long cooldown periods, I thought it hadn’t reactivated yet.

But—

“He has consumed the Hellase Essence.”

Hellase’s passive is a very peculiar survival skill.

I did notice that he loves to show off. So he was just a coward.
