# Raid 4



As soon as I raised my hammer, I activated [Transcendence] and [Eye of the Storm], pulling the dagger user towards me.

Swaaaaaaaaaa!

The ambush was successful. His frail body was helplessly dragged towards me.

"......!"

I reached out and grabbed his neck.

At that moment, Amelia called out to me.

"Yandel."

Her voice was filled with worry.

If she thought I was actually going to kill him, I was a little offended.

I wouldn't crush his head over something like this.

I'm not a real barbarian.

Squeeze.

This was just a performance.

A slightly... emotional one.

"Mister, they're almost here."

As expected, as soon as I grabbed his neck, the other explorers appeared in the distance. It didn't take long for them to reach us.

"...What is the meaning of this, Baron Yandel?!"

They already knew my name.

It wasn't surprising.

They must have been listening to our conversation through a message stone or something.

"Cough...!"

I tightened my grip, and the dagger user struggled even more.

"Release him this instant!"

"Why should I?"

"Even if you're a noble, there are lines you cannot cross! How dare you threaten an innocent explorer without any reason?! Do you think this will be tolerated?!"

His words were absurd.

First of all, they were the ones who had summoned the Floor Lord without permission.

And secondly...

"Who said he was innocent?"

I spoke clearly, loud enough for everyone to hear.

"He's a traitor. He dared to threaten and insult a noble of Lafdonia!"

"W-What?! That's ridiculous! He would never—"

"What? Are you insulting me too?"

"N-No, that's not what I meant..."

The dagger user struggled even more as our conversation continued.

"Cough... Urgh...!"

He was quite persistent.

He should have drawn his dagger and stabbed me by now.

'He knows that would only make things worse.'

"L-Let's talk this over. There must be some misunderstanding. We can resolve this peacefully if we just—"

I didn't think he would attack me, even if I provoked him further.

So I threw him back to his comrades.

"V-Vice-Captain! Are you alright?"

Huh... he was the vice-captain?

The one who replaced James Kala.

'No wonder he was so persistent...'

His loyalty to the clan was admirable, but that didn't make me like him or the Sawtooth Clan any more.

"Alright, explain yourselves. What was the misunderstanding?"

I asked the explorer who had been shouting at me, but the dagger user intervened, covering his mouth.

And...

"We sincerely apologize for disturbing Baron Yandel. Please forgive us."

He apologized without any excuses.

"Mentioning Count Alminus was a grave mistake on our part."

He was a sharp one.

He knew exactly how to avoid the traps I had laid.
