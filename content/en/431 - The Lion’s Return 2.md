# The Lion’s Return 2

The sky was somewhat gloomy.

Even so, sunlight still streamed through.

A warm energy pushed back the chill that had seeped into our very bones.

There was no bone-chilling coldness, nor hunger gnawing at our stomachs.

“Now… it’s finally sinking in. We really… made it back alive…”

As always, returning to the city after a difficult expedition brought strange sensations.

We were happy to have survived, yet our hearts felt heavy.

Because of those who couldn’t come back with us.

“If only they had endured a little longer…”

If only…

Maybe a few more could have joined us.

But…

Clench.

She swallowed her complaints.

It wasn’t just because we were currently in the empty mansion where we had gathered for a few days before entering the labyrinth and had to be careful of what we said.

We couldn’t afford to complain.

No matter how high and steep the mountains we had to climb were,

We had to leave those who fought to the very end behind.

“What? Am I wrong?!”

Sven Parab looked at me nervously, but it wasn’t something I had to intervene in.

Their complaints were understandable.

And besides, I had told them to do this.

“Ah, thank you.”

I took the plate of meat from Erwen and started eating, my mind replaying the events of the past few days.

I had to make sure there weren’t any mistakes in our story.

‘…The checkpoint guards didn’t seem to know anything.’

As soon as we returned to the Dimension Plaza, we stored our backpacks in our Bonding subspace pockets.

We had carefully sorted the items we could show them and the ones we couldn’t.

And then we arrived at the checkpoint.

I declared that we were a royal special forces unit and demanded to speak with the Marquis.

It was intentional.

‘The checkpoint was crowded with explorers, so the rumors about the special forces unit must have spread by now.’

The checkpoint official was flustered, but he just kept repeating the Marquis’s orders, telling us to wait.

And then…

[We’ll escort you from here.]

The knights from the Marquis’s family arrived within 30 minutes.

They escorted us to the empty mansion in the Imperial City, the one where the founding ceremony was held.

[We’ll carry your luggage.]

[Our luggage…?]

[It’s the Marquis’s order. We swear on our honor as knights that nothing will be lost.]

They even took the backpacks containing the loot we could show them.

And then we received healing from the priests they summoned.

And that brings us to the present.

Knock, knock.

We hear a knock on the door.

Right, it’s about time.

They’ve finally finished assessing the situation.

“The Marquis wishes to see you all.”

Let’s be careful.
